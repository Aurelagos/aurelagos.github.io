AI Trading Coach & Portfolio Dashboard

  • Python
  • Claude API
  • Brokerage APIs
  • HTML dashboard

A personal dashboard that pulls portfolio data from brokerage APIs and uses an LLM "coach" to review changes in positions. Instead of just showing numbers, it asks whether a move fits the investing framework it was meant to follow.

Highlights

  • Risk lens: a four-factor review (leverage, convexity, asymmetry, reflexivity) applied to each position change.
  • Event-driven reviews: coach reviews trigger automatically when positions change, and routine events such as expirations are handled separately to avoid unnecessary API calls.
  • Framework-aware: reviews are anchored to a portfolio structure with explicit sleeves and rules, so feedback is consistent rather than generic.
  • Hygiene: secrets kept in environment files, and public data sources used where a paid feed is not needed.

Built for personal use. Holdings, account data, and credentials are not part of this portfolio.

P&C Insurance Market Research Workflow

  • Claude
  • Source verification
  • Synthesis
  • Industry analysis

A repeatable human-plus-AI research process for tracking the property & casualty market. I collect primary sources such as trade press and industry commentary, and the AI helps verify, contextualize, and fold them into a structured, evolving view of the market.

How it works

  • Verify before integrating: each claim is checked, and anything that cannot be independently confirmed is labeled as such.
  • Facts vs. inference: confirmed facts are kept separate from well-reasoned inferences, and attribution is flagged when sources conflict.
  • Iterative build: an initial structured overview, then successive rounds of new sources.

Themes explored

  • Where the market cycle differs by line of business, and how quickly those conditions change.
  • How regional regulation reshapes carrier appetite in residential property.
  • Why value in insurance technology tends to accumulate in the layer that owns data and context.
  • The lag between soft-market pricing and the underwriting results that follow.
